03 — Process

How a project runs

Four stages, fixed checkpoints, no disappearing acts. You see work every week and you always know what happens next.

01

Listen

A working session on the business: what you sell, who buys it, what has stalled. We write it down and agree the one outcome this project is judged on.

02

Map

Sitemap, content plan, and wireframes. Cheap to change at this stage, expensive later — so this is where we argue about it.

03

Craft

Design and build in the open. A live staging link from week one, updated as it grows, with review rounds baked into the schedule.

04

Launch & tend

Testing, analytics, handover training, go-live. Then a care plan if you want one — updates, backups, and small improvements every month.

04 — How we work

Promises we keep

A young studio has to be clearer than an old one. These are the terms we hold ourselves to on every engagement.

24h

Maximum reply time on any working day — even if the answer is “still looking into it”.

100%

Ownership handed to you. Code, domains, analytics and hosting sit in your accounts, not ours.

0

Off-the-shelf templates used. Every layout is designed for the content it carries.

1

Point of contact from first call to launch. No account-manager relay race.

Fixed scope

Priced before we start

You get a written proposal with deliverables, stages and a total. If scope changes, we quote the change — nothing appears on an invoice by surprise.

Performance

Built to load fast

Lean pages, compressed images, and no plugin sprawl. Speed is a design decision, and slow sites lose customers before they read a word.

Accessibility

Usable by everyone

Keyboard navigation, real contrast ratios, sensible headings and alt text as standard — not an add-on line item.
